    Share?

    Choose Share, then Copy link to send a snapshot of your text. Opening it loads the text into Plainlet. Later edits won’t change a link you already shared.

    The text is compressed inside the link. Anyone with an ordinary link can read it. Plainlet does not upload your text.

    For private sharing, turn on Password protect. Type a strong password or choose Generate, then copy the link and password separately. Recipients need both. Plainlet does not save passwords and cannot recover a forgotten one.

    Links do not expire and cannot be revoked. They may be saved in browser history or by the app you send them through.

    Links are limited to 8,000 characters and 1 MiB of text before compression. Some apps may shorten or break long links. If a paste is too big to share, you can still paste, edit, and copy it normally.

    Privacy

    Plainlet is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no server-side storage for your text.

    Your current text clears when you reload or close the page. Pastes are saved in this browser’s local history until you delete them individually or choose Clear history. Copying puts text on your device’s clipboard.

    Only app files are cached on your device for offline use. Plainlet does not upload your text.

    Share links contain a snapshot of your text. Anyone with an ordinary link can read it. Optional password protection encrypts the snapshot; its password stays outside the link and is not saved by Plainlet. Links may be kept in browser history or messaging apps; they cannot be revoked.

    Install on device

    iPhone or iPad
    1. Open Plainlet in Safari.
    2. Tap Share (the square with an up arrow). You may need to tap the … menu first.
    3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If missing, find it under Edit Actions.
    4. Turn on Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.
    Android
    1. Open Plainlet in Chrome.
    2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to home screen or Install app.
    3. Choose Install if offered and follow the prompts.
    Computer

    In Chrome or Edge, look for Install in the address bar or browser menu. On a Mac in Safari, choose File → Add to Dock.
